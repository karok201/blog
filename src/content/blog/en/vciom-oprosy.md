title: VTsIOM and their (not so) useless polls
description: Why I always answer VTsIOM calls, take their surveys and vote in elections.
date: 2026-08-17
tags: [society]
---

I always try to answer their calls and take the survey. I genuinely believe that if I have even the slightest chance - even a virtual one, even one that exists only in my head - to influence something in the country, I will take it: answer these polls and vote in elections. Plus, I recently read in a book ([link](https://www.moscowbooks.ru/book/1222949/)) that opinion polls are one of the few tools our establishment actually uses to adjust the country's course.

First, it helps me grow the part of myself that belongs to civil society and can make a difference. Second, if there is even a small chance that my answer or my vote will be counted, I will give it.

I have no illusions about the falsifications, ballot stuffing and all the other ugliness that will inevitably come with the vote in a month. Still, I consider it my duty to prepare myself - and, where I can, the people around me - for the day when we live in a country (or don't) that has a civil society.

That is what I call on myself and on you to do.
